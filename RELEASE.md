### What's new

Show how you wrote it.

**Writing history.** A "writing history" button in the status bar opens a record of how a project grew: every saved version with its date and exactly what changed, the stretches of time you spent typing, and any larger text you pasted in. Read it in words or line by line, then save it as a text file or a PDF for a teacher. Your name and course go at the top, and the record explains how anyone can check it against the project's real history and GitHub's own record of when each version arrived.

**The library fills in after a sync.** Documents and projects that arrived from another device used to appear only after restarting Zerkalo. Now they show up within seconds, labels and all.

**Easier to read.** Comfort settings and the word count window no longer cut words off or squeeze them into a few letters per line, and Recent messages is easier on the eye.

**Backs up every 5 minutes** while there are changes, so a history has finer steps. It still says nothing unless a backup doesn't go through.
