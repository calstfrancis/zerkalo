### What's new

Your writing follows you between computers.

**Get Latest from GitHub** (☰ menu) brings everything backed up online down to this computer — work in progress included — and sends nothing back. Edits made here are saved as a version first, so nothing is lost, and if the same part of a document changed in both places Zerkalo changes nothing and tells you.

**Start-up catch-up.** A computer that is simply behind updates itself when Zerkalo opens; if you have work here that isn't backed up yet, you're offered *Get it* instead.

**New computers.** The setup wizard now brings your writing down when you point it at an existing online copy.
