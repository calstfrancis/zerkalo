### What's new

Spelling, one click away.

**A "spelling" button in the status bar.** It sits right after "search" and opens the step-through spell check, which shows each misspelled word in its sentence and lets you change it, skip it, or add it to the dictionary. It works the same as Shift+F7 and needs no underlines on screen.
