### What's new

A spell check you can walk through, and an underline you can see in dark mode.

**Shift+F7 steps through every misspelled word.** A small window shows each one in its sentence, selects it in the editor, and offers suggestions. Change it, change every one in the document, skip it, skip every one, or add it to your dictionary or the project's; Back returns to the one before. It works even when spell check is switched off or set to "only when I ask". You can also find it as "Spell check…" in the command palette.

**A clearer underline in dark mode.** The wavy underline is a brighter blue on dark pages, a touch deeper on light ones, and follows the theme when you switch.
