### What's new

A calmer, more predictable Zerkalo.

**It does what the label says.** Ctrl+Shift+P updates the preview instead of hiding it. Ctrl+S saves exactly as safely as ☰ → Save, and tells you if a save can't go through. Help points at the real backup button, and every shortcut shown on screen is the one you have set.

**It keeps quiet while you write.** No message for an autosave or an automatic backup that worked. An update leaves a small note in the status bar rather than opening a window. Newer writing from your online backup asks before it changes what is on screen. Every message stays long enough to read, and the last thirty can be read again from the "Saved" line at the bottom.

**One name for one thing.** Backing up, saved versions and updating the preview are called the same everywhere. Labels are in sentence case, and a switch's tooltip says whether it is on and what a click will do.

**Settings → Comfort** gathers the choices: reduced movement, a steady cursor, a quiet colour for problems, status-bar numbers that settle when you pause, spelling that leaves the word you are still writing alone (F7 checks everything), a "where you left off" line, notes for next time, a mark on your place after a break, and an optional session clock.
